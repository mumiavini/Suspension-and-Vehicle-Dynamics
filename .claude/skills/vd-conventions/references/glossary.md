# Vehicle dynamics glossary

| Term (EN) | Symbol | Unit | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Camber | γ | deg | Angle of wheel plane vs vertical in the front view. Negative = top inboard. |
| Caster | τ | deg | Rearward tilt of the kingpin axis in the side view. |
| Kingpin inclination (KPI) | σ | deg | Inboard tilt of the kingpin axis in the front view. |
| Scrub radius | rs | mm | Lateral distance from kingpin-ground intercept to contact patch centre, in the front view. |
| Mechanical trail | tm | mm | Longitudinal distance from kingpin-ground intercept to contact patch centre, in the side view. |
| Pneumatic trail | tp | mm | Distance from contact patch centre to the centroid of the lateral force distribution. Load- and slip-dependent. |
| Toe (per side) | δtoe | deg | Angle of the wheel centreline vs the vehicle centreline in plan view. Positive = toe-in. Always state per-side or total. |
| Roll centre height | hRC | mm | Height of the point about which the sprung mass rolls relative to the unsprung mass, in the front view. |
| Instant centre (IC) | — | — | The point about which the contact patch instantaneously rotates in a given view (front or side). |
| Front-view swing arm (FVSA) | — | mm | Length of the virtual arm from the contact patch to the front-view instant centre. |
| Side-view swing arm (SVSA) | — | mm | Length of the virtual arm from the contact patch to the side-view instant centre. |
| Anti-dive | %AD | % | Percentage of braking-induced pitch absorbed by suspension geometry. |
| Anti-squat | %AS | % | Percentage of acceleration-induced squat absorbed by suspension geometry. |
| Anti-lift | %AL | % | Percentage of braking-induced front lift absorbed by rear suspension geometry. |
| Wheelbase | L | mm | Longitudinal distance between front and rear axle centrelines. |
| Track width | T | mm | Lateral distance between left and right contact patch centres on the same axle. |
| Steering ratio | — | :1 | Ratio of steering wheel angle to average road wheel angle. |
| Ackermann | — | % | Percentage of geometric Ackermann achieved by the steering geometry. |
| Bump steer | — | deg/mm | Change in toe angle per unit of wheel travel. |
| Camber gain (ride) | — | deg/mm | Change in camber per unit of vertical wheel travel (heave). |
| Roll camber | — | deg/deg | Change in camber per degree of body roll. |
| Motion ratio | MR | — | Ratio of spring/damper displacement to wheel displacement. |
| Wheel rate | kw | N/mm | Effective spring rate at the wheel centre (= spring rate × MR²). |
